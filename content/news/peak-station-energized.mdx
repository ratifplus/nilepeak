Tonight, for the first time, a Nile Peak station is holding the lights on by itself.

The Peak Station is a grid-forming battery power station. When the grid is strong, it charges quietly. When supply drops, it does not wait to be told. It forms the grid itself, holds frequency steady, and keeps homes, clinics and workshops running as if nothing happened.

## What changes for the neighbourhood

Nothing visible. That is the point. Fridges keep humming. Water pumps keep pushing. Shops stay open past dark. The station is designed so that the people it serves never have to think about it.

## What comes next

We are learning from every hour of operation. Each night of data makes the next station faster to build and smarter to run. We will share more as we can.
