Nile Peak has been formed to develop grid-forming battery stations for national power grids.

Our stations store energy, hold frequency and voltage steady, and restart the grid after a blackout. We develop them with utilities, governments, financiers and engineering contractors.

Further announcements will follow as projects reach agreed milestones.
