Nile Peak is building critical infrastructure. During our first deployments we will share what our stations do for the people they serve, and keep the details of where and how they are built to ourselves.

This is a deliberate choice. It protects our partners, our sites and the communities around them while our first stations come online.

## What we will share

- What the station does, and how it feels on the ground.
- How the technology works, in principle.
- News about our team, our partners and our progress, as partners agree.

## What we will not share, for now

Site locations, capacities, grid connection details and supplier relationships.

Accredited media and prospective partners can request a briefing through the contact page.
